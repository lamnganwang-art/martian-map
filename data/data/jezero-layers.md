# Jezero Crater Map Layers

## 1. Surface imagery
- What it shows: Orbital images of the surface, including terrain, rocks, and landscape features.
- Why it matters: Helps identify possible routes, science targets, and visible terrain features.
- Source: USGS Mars 2020 Jezero Crater landing-site products.
- Link: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars-2020-jezero-crater-landing-site-products

## 2. Terrain and elevation
- What it shows: Elevation and terrain shape around Jezero Crater.
- Why it matters: Helps understand slopes, valleys, and areas that may be difficult to cross.
- Source: USGS CTX digital elevation model mosaic.
- Link: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars-2020-jezero-crater-landing-site-products

## 3. Geology
- What it shows: Different geologic units, such as crater-floor rocks, delta deposits, and surrounding terrain.
- Why it matters: Helps identify scientifically interesting locations for a Marswalk.
- Source: USGS Geologic Map of Jezero Crater and the Nili Planum Region.
- Link: https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/sim3464
