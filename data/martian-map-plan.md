# Martian Map Plan

## Goal
Create a layered Mars map for Jezero Crater that helps a future astronaut:
1. Understand the terrain.
2. Identify scientific places to visit.
3. Avoid or prepare for hazards.
4. Plan a safe Marswalk route.

## Chosen site
Jezero Crater, Mars.

## Layers we will show
1. Surface imagery
2. Terrain and elevation
3. Geology
4. Radiation awareness
5. Dust and visibility
6. Temperature
7. Wind
8. Atmospheric pressure
9. Terrain risk

## Science targets
1. Delta front
2. Crater floor
3. Crater margin

## Hazards
1. Delta slopes
2. Rocky crater floor
3. Dust and visibility
4. Radiation and space weather
5. Temperature extremes

## What the map will show
- A map of Jezero Crater.
- Toggle buttons for each layer.
- Pins for science targets.
- Colored warning areas for hazards.
- A route from a habitat/start point to a science target and back.
