# Jezero Crater Hazard Notes

## 1. Delta slopes
- Hazard: Slopes and uneven terrain near the delta.
- Why it matters: Climbing may be physically difficult for an astronaut.
- Planning response: Mark as “caution” and look for gentler access routes.

## 2. Rocky crater floor
- Hazard: Rocks and rough terrain.
- Why it matters: May make walking and vehicle travel harder.
- Planning response: Use imagery and terrain data to identify smoother paths.

## 3. Dust and visibility
- Hazard: Martian dust can reduce visibility and affect equipment.
- Why it matters: An astronaut needs clear visibility and reliable tools during a Marswalk.
- Planning response: Include weather and dust context before finalizing a route.
