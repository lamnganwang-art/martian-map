# Martian Map — Site Notes

## Project goal
We are creating a layered Mars map to help a future astronaut plan a safe Marswalk
and visit scientifically interesting locations.

## Chosen site
Jezero Crater, Mars.

## Why Jezero Crater?
Jezero Crater is the landing and exploration area of NASA’s Perseverance rover.
It contains evidence of an ancient lake and river-delta environment.

## Places to investigate
1. Delta area  
2. Crater floor  
3. Crater rim  

## Next steps
- Find NASA imagery and terrain information for Jezero Crater.
- Identify 3 science targets.
- Identify possible hazards or difficult terrain.
