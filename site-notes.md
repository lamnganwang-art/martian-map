# Martian Map — Site Notes

## Project goal
We are creating a layered Mars map to help a future astronaut plan a safe Marswalk
and visit scientifically interesting locations.

## Chosen site
Jezero Crater, Mars.

## Why Jezero Crater?
Jezero Crater is the landing and exploration area of NASA’s Perseverance rover.
It contains evidence of an ancient lake and river-delta environment.

## Places to investigate
1. Delta area  
2. Crater floor  
3. Crater rim

## Science targets

### 1. Delta front
- What it is: The lower edge of the ancient river delta.
- Why visit: Layered sedimentary rocks may preserve evidence of ancient water and environmental conditions.
- Astronaut activity: Observe layers, take images, and collect samples.

### 2. Crater floor
- What it is: The area inside Jezero Crater.
- Why visit: Perseverance found igneous rocks that interacted with water.
- Astronaut activity: Compare rock types and study how water changed the rocks.

### 3. Crater margin
- What it is: The area near the canyon entrance where a river may have entered the crater.
- Why visit: It can help explain how water entered and filled Jezero Crater.
- Astronaut activity: Examine the landscape and identify evidence of ancient water flow.
